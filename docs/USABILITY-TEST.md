# Cooking usability test: script and observation sheet

**Status: not run yet.** Nothing in the repo describes a real person cooking with Reelish. Fill in the sheet below
after a session; don't summarise results that weren't observed.

## Setup (10 minutes before)

- **Participant:** someone who cooks at least occasionally and hasn't seen Reelish. Not you.
- **Device:** *their* phone, in *their* kitchen if possible. Charge it first.
- **Recipe:** pick one they'd actually make, with ingredients on hand. Good choices from the evaluation:
  - **Pasta salad** (Instagram, caption recipe, ~25 min, needs an air fryer or oven):
    `https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWZqrugEacU/`
  - **Creamy vegetable soup** (YouTube Short, ~40 min): `https://www.youtube.com/shorts/VTR8vxBFGDY`
- **Optional second, short task:** a partial recipe (the Kaju Short, `https://www.youtube.com/shorts/s4na199iyag`)
  just to look at, not cook. It tests how "Not provided" is understood.
- **Before they arrive:** open the recipe once yourself, so it's cached and costs nothing during the session.
- **For you:** this sheet, a timer, and a way to note times. Ask before taking any photos.

## What to say (read it out)

> "This is an early app that turns cooking videos into recipes. I'm testing the app, not you. Please think
> aloud: say what you're looking for and what surprises you. I won't help unless you're stuck for a while,
> because I want to see where the app falls short."

## Tasks

1. **"Here's a link to a video. Get the recipe."** Send the link by message. Don't explain how to paste it.
2. **"Before you start, check you've got everything."** (The overview and checklist.)
3. **"Change one amount to what you'll actually use."** Only if it comes up naturally, or ask once they're done
   prepping.
4. **"Now cook it using the app."** Don't prompt. Watch.
5. *(Optional)* **Partial recipe:** "What would you do with this one?"
6. **Debrief (5 min):**
   - "What was the most annoying moment?"
   - "Was anything on screen wrong or confusing?"
   - "Would you use this next time instead of the video? Why or why not?"
   - "What did 'Not provided' / 'Estimated' mean to you?"
